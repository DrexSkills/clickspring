1. Who we are

ClickSpring (“ClickSpring”, “we”, “us”) is an independent product run from Kentucky, United States. ClickSpring helps website owners understand why visitors don’t become customers. You can reach us through our contact form.

This policy covers two groups of people: account holders, who sign up to use ClickSpring, and visitors to websites that use ClickSpring. For visitor data, the website owner decides what is collected and why; ClickSpring processes it on their behalf (in GDPR terms, the website owner is the “controller” and ClickSpring is the “processor”; under US state laws, ClickSpring acts as a “service provider”).

Cookies and browser storage

ClickSpring doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies. When you use the ClickSpring website and app, we store a few things in your browser’s local storage:

ItemTypeWhy
Sign-in session (sb-…-auth-token)EssentialKeeps you signed in. Removed when you log out.
App settings (for example cs_site_…, cs_tour_…)EssentialRemembers which website you’re viewing and whether you’ve seen the tour.

ClickSpring analytics (cs_vid, cs_src, cs_camp)OptionalWe use ClickSpring on our own website to see which pages people visit and where they leave, with a random ID and no cookies or stored IP address. Only if you choose “Accept all”. We don’t track you inside the app after you log in.

You can change your choice any time with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of our pages, or by clearing this site’s data in your browser. The ClickSpring tracking script that our customers put on their websites works differently and is described in section 3.

We also keep an anonymous count of how many times our pages are loaded: one number per page per day, with no ID, no cookie and no IP address. This runs whatever you choose in the cookie notice, and it can’t be traced back to you.

3. Data about visitors to our customers’ websites

When a website uses the ClickSpring script, ClickSpring records:

  • A random visitor ID stored in the browser’s local storage, so visits by the same browser can be grouped. It is not linked to a name or email by ClickSpring.
  • Pages viewed (the page path, without the rest of the URL), clicks on buttons and links (the visible button text, which section of the page it is in, how far down the page it sits, and where it links), and sign-up events (that a sign-up happened, not what was typed).
  • Time on page and scrolling: how many seconds someone stayed on a page and how far down they scrolled, recorded once when they leave it.
  • Where the visit came from: a general source such as “Instagram” or “Google”, based on the link or referring site, and the website owner’s tracking link if one was used.
  • Device type: phone, tablet or computer, and the time of each event.

What we don’t collect: we don’t use cookies, we don’t store IP addresses with visitor events, we don’t record keystrokes or form contents, we don’t take screenshots or recordings, and we don’t follow people across different websites.

Opting out: the ClickSpring script does nothing if the visitor’s browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal. Visitors can also clear their browser’s site data to reset their random ID, or contact the website owner, who can delete their data.

4. How we use data

  • To provide ClickSpring: dashboards, Why They Leave, visitor journeys, plans and the API.
  • To keep the service secure and working, and to fix problems.
  • To send account and service emails. We don’t send marketing emails without your permission.
  • To improve ClickSpring using overall usage patterns.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We don’t sell personal data, we don’t “share” it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we don’t use visitor data from one customer’s website for any other customer.

Legal bases (for people in the EU/UK): performing our contract with account holders; our legitimate interests in running a secure, working service; and, for visitor data, the legal basis chosen by the website owner.

6. How long we keep it

We keep account data and website data for as long as your account is active. When you delete a website or your account in Settings, its data, including all visitor events, tracking links, changes and API keys, is deleted from our database right away. Copies in our providers’ backups are removed on their normal backup cycle.

7. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws like the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict some processing, and to not be discriminated against for using these rights.

  • Account holders: edit your details in Settings, export your data with the API, and delete your account in Settings → Delete account. You can also email us.
  • Visitors to a website that uses ClickSpring: please contact that website’s owner first, since they control the data. If you contact us, we’ll pass your request to them and help them respond.

We’ll respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), row-level access rules so each account can only reach its own data, hashed passwords and API keys, and limited access for the public tracking key (it can only add events, never read them). No system is perfectly secure, so we can’t guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your data, we’ll notify you as required by law.

9. International transfers

ClickSpring is run from the United States and stores data in the United States. If you use ClickSpring from elsewhere, your data will be transferred to and processed in the United States, with safeguards required by applicable law.

10. Children

ClickSpring accounts are for people 18 and older. ClickSpring is not directed at children, and website owners must not use it on sites or pages directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU/UK). If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we’ll delete it.

11. For website owners: text for your privacy policy

If you use ClickSpring on your website, you must tell your visitors. You can adapt this text for your own privacy policy:

We use ClickSpring to understand how visitors use our website so we can improve it. ClickSpring records the pages you view, how long you stay and how far you scroll, the buttons and links you click and where they are on the page, whether you sign up, the general source of your visit (for example Instagram or Google) and your device type, under a random ID stored in your browser’s local storage. It does not use cookies, does not record what you type, and does not store your IP address with this data. ClickSpring processes this data on our behalf and does not sell it. To opt out, enable Global Privacy Control in your browser or clear this site’s data. Learn more at https://clickspring.live/privacy.html

In the EU, UK and some other places, you may need visitors’ consent before analytics run. If so, load the ClickSpring script only after the visitor agrees.

12. Changes and contact

If we make important changes, we’ll update the date above and tell account holders by email or in the app before the changes take effect. Questions or requests: use our contact form.
